ABOUT THE STATION

Nothing you were meant to hear.

topsecret.fm is an independent internet radio station mixing rare music, news satire, and long-form talk — broadcasting on a frequency nobody officially cleared.

Meet the crate diggers

Who's behind the console, and how the archive gets built one estate sale at a time. Our diggers are all anonymous and go through an enhanced Music Vetting process. Are you interested in digging? Get in touch.

How we broadcast

No filter

We say the thing. Satire only lands when nobody's holding back.

No schedule

Segments surface when they're ready, not on a programming grid.

No permission

If it's obscure enough to need a search warrant, it's in rotation.

No boring

If a segment isn't earning its airtime, it gets cut. Simple as that.

Licensing & rights

Properly licensed, obscure or not

topsecret.fm streams under a PRS for Music Internet Radio Licence and a PPL Linear Webcast Licence, covering the composition and recording rights for the music we play in the UK. Rare doesn't mean unlicensed.

Got a lead, a tape, or a story that belongs on Debrief? Reach out — hello@topsecret.fm.
